The Aahaas Auto Call Bot is an AI-powered WhatsApp voice calling assistant integrated with the Apple Holidays Travel Booking System.
The main purpose of this component is to automatically communicate with the lead passenger during an ongoing trip, collect real-time travel feedback, identify service issues, and improve the customer experience.
The bot has complete knowledge about each specific booking

Using this information, the bot creates personalized conversations instead of asking generic feedback questions.

First Day Travel Experience Call
Trigger: First night of the arrival/travel start date
The system automatically schedules a WhatsApp AI voice call to the lead passenger.
The bot identifies the first-day itinerary before calling.


Mid-Trip Feedback Calls
The bot does not call the passenger every day to avoid disturbing the customer.
The system follows scheduled interval calls.
Example:
Day 1 → First experience call
Day 3 → Feedback call covering Day 2 and Day 3 experience
Day 5 → Feedback call covering Day 4 and Day 5 experience
This process continues until the departure date.
