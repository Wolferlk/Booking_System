Subject: Introducing OPS-AI — the operations copilot now live in our booking system

Dear [Boss's name],

I would like to introduce a capability we have built into the AppleHolidays 
OPS-AI, an operations copilot that works alongside our team inside the
system they already use every day.

What it is
OPS-AI is not a separate app or a generic chatbot. It is an assistant embedded
directly in the platform that understands our bookings, our workflow and our
rules across all four countries. Staff open it with a single keystroke on any
screen and ask for what they need in plain English — by typing or by voice — and
it responds with the answer, opens the right page, or drafts the exact change to
a booking for one-click approval.

What it can do
It has 11 built-in actions, in three groups:
  • Look up  — search bookings and read full booking detail, instantly.
  • Navigate — open a booking or jump to a filtered list (e.g. all arrivals on a
    given date).
  • Change   — draft edits to booking fields, tour agenda items, accommodation
    and driver assignments, and create reminders or log customer contacts.

A few real examples of what a staff member can simply say:
  • "Add a full-day Sigiriya sightseeing tour on day 3 of IS23492."
  • "Which bookings arrive on 1 July?"
  • "Assign driver Nuwan, van CAB-4471, to the airport pickup on this booking."
  • "Remind me to chase the balance payment on this file next Monday." (by voice)

In each case OPS-AI does the heavy lifting and presents a clear preview card;
the staff member confirms with one click.

Why it is safe
Because this is a live system with real booking and financial records, safety was
the starting point. Every action passes five mandatory checks, and — most
importantly — OPS-AI never acts on its own. It proposes; a person confirms, and
every change is written to the audit trail. It has no ability to delete or cancel
a booking, change a booking's status, confirm payments, or manage users. Each
person only ever reaches bookings for their own country and role, and OPS-AI
inherits exactly those limits.

Why it matters
The everyday win is time and consistency. Routine jobs that today mean moving
across several screens and forms become a single instruction, with the same
guardrails applied to everyone, every time.
